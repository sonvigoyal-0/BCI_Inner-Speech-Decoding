# Problem Statement

This section is formed to document the key research problems identified after reviewing the recent literature review on EEG-based imagined speech Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs).

Most existing studies claimed for classification performance on individual datasets. However, several practical challenges still limit the development of robust, low-latency, and deployable imagined speech BCIs.

Based on our literature review, we identified two major research problems that consistently appear across existing work.

## Problem 1
Lack of a Standardized Benchmark Framework

## Problem 2
Limited Generalization Across Subjects, Sessions, and Datasets

These problems serve as the foundation for exploring robust methodologies that can improve the reliability and practical deployment of imagined speech decoding systems.
